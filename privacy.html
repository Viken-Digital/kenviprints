Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy covers KenviPrints Pin Publisher (the app) and this website. KenviPrints is a small Etsy shop that sells printable digital products.

What the app does

The app is used only by KenviPrints. It connects to our own Pinterest business account, KenviPrints, through the Pinterest API. It creates pins that show our products and link to our Etsy listings. It may also read our own boards and pins, to check that a pin was created correctly.

What data the app uses

  • The app is run only by the KenviPrints shop owner, for the KenviPrints business account. It accesses only that account's data: our boards, our pins, and the text, pictures and links we add to them.
  • The app does not sign in other Pinterest users and does not access their data. It does not sell or share any data.
  • The app does not save copies of our boards or pins. It reads them only while it runs, to check that a pin was created correctly.
  • The only thing the app keeps is the sign-in token Pinterest gives us. It is stored only on the shop owner's computer, is not shared with anyone, and is deleted when we stop using the app.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Removing access

The app's access to our Pinterest account can be removed at any time in the Pinterest account's settings.

Purchases

Orders are placed on Etsy. Etsy's own privacy policy covers buyers' data: etsy.com/legal/privacy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Questions: send us a message through our Etsy shop.
