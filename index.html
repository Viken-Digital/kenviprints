KenviPrints Pin Publisher

KenviPrints Pin Publisher is a small tool we use only for our own shop. It creates pins on our own Pinterest business account, KenviPrints. Each pin shows one of our products and links to its page in our Etsy shop.

The tool is not offered to anyone else. It does not sign in other Pinterest users, and it does not read or collect their data.
